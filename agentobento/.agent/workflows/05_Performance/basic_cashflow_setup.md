# 05.2 Basic Cashflow Setup

**Goal:** Don't run out of money.

## 👤 Human Steps
1.  **Separate Accounts:** Ensure Business and Personal checking are 100% separate.
2.  **The "Profit First" Setup:** (Optional) Open sub-accounts for Tax, Profit, and OpEx.
3.  **Tooling:** Connect bank feeds to Xero/Quickbooks (or a simple Google Sheet).

## 🤖 AI Agent Steps
1.  **Expense Audit:** (If data provided) Categorize last month's spend and identify subscriptions to cancel.

## 📄 Deliverable
-   A "Flash Report" showing Cash on Hand vs. Burn Rate.
