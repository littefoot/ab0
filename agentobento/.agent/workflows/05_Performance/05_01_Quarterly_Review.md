# Operations: Quarterly Business Review (QBR)

**Goal:** Retain the client by showing progress and planning the future.

## 🤖 AI Agent Steps
1.  **Data Analysis:** (If integrated) Pull analytics from reports.
2.  **Presentation Prep:** Generate a slide deck outline:
    -   *Look Back:* What we achieved vs. goals.
    -   *The Data:* KPI review.
    -   *Look Forward:* Next quarter's roadmap.

## 👤 Human Steps
1.  **Review Strategy:** Has the business changed? Update the `01_Strategy/roadmap.json` accordingly.
2.  **The Meeting:** Present the deck. Re-sign or upsell.
