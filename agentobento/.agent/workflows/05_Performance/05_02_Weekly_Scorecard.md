# 05.02 The Weekly Scorecard

**Goal:** A "Monday Morning" pulse check. No surprises.

## Metric Categories

### 1. Financial Health (The Oxygen)
-   **Cash on Hand:** (Bank Balance).
-   **Burn Rate:** (Monthly Expenses).
-   **Runway:** (Months left).

### 2. Growth Workflow (The Inputs)
-   **Traffic/Impressions:** (Volume).
-   **Leads Generated:** (Conversion).
-   **Sales Calls Booked:** (Output).

### 3. Fulfillment Workflow (The Outputs)
-   **Projects Completed:** (Volume).
-   **NPS / Happiness Score:** (Quality).
-   **Churn Rate:** (Retention).

## 👤 Human Steps
1.  **The Ritual:** Every Monday at 9:00 AM.
2.  **Statusing:**
    -   🟢 **Green:** On track.
    -   🟡 **Yellow:** Behind but distinct plan to fix.
    -   🔴 **Red:** Behind and need help/resources.
