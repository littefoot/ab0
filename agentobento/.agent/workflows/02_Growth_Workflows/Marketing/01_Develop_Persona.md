# Marketing Strategy: Develop Persona

**Goal:** Define exactly who we are selling to.

## 🤖 AI Agent Steps
1.  **Analyze Content:** Read `00_Discovery/dossier.md` and any provided client content.
2.  **Generate Avatar:** Create a markdown profile for "Customer X":
    -   *Demographics:* Age, Location, Job.
    -   *Psychographics:* Fears, Dreams, secret desires.
    -   *The "Before" State:* Life with the problem.
    -   *The "After" State:* Life with the solution.

## 👤 Human Steps
1.  **Review:** Does this feel like a real person?
2.  **Save:** `02_Assets/Marketing/01_Strategy/avatar_[name].md`
