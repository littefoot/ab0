# Marketing: Email Sequence Setup

**Goal:** Nurture leads into buyers automatically.

## 🤖 AI Agent Steps
1.  **Framework Selection:** Suggest "Soap Opera Sequence" (Story-based) or "Seinfeld Sequence" (Entertainment-based).
2.  **Drafting:** Write 5 emails:
    -   *Email 1:* The "Stage Setter" (Welcome + Usage of Lead Magnet).
    -   *Email 2:* The "Drama" (Backstory of the problem).
    -   *Email 3:* The "Epiphany" (Discovery of the solution).
    -   *Email 4:* The "Hidden Benefits" (Logic).
    -   *Email 5:* The "Urgency/Scarcity" (Push to buy).

## 👤 Human Steps
1.  **Tone Check:** Ensure it sounds like the client, not a robot.
2.  **Setup:** Load into Mailchimp/ConvertKit/ActiveCampaign.
3.  **Save:** `02_Assets/Marketing/03_Email_Sequences/welcome_series.md`
