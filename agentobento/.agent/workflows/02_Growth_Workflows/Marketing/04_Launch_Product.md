# Marketing: Launch Entry Product

**Goal:** Convert leads into customers with a trusted, low-risk offer ($27-$97).

## 🤖 AI Agent Steps
1.  **Offer Structure:** Define the "Stack".
    -   Core Product (Video/E-book).
    -   Bonus 1 (Checklist).
    -   Bonus 2 (Case Study).
2.  **Sales Letter:** Draft a long-form sales page structure (Headline, Lead, Bullets, Guarantee, CTA).

## 👤 Human Steps
1.  **Build:** Create the product content.
2.  **Tech Stack:** Setup Stripe link or checkout page.
3.  **Save:** `02_Assets/Marketing/06_Entry_Products/product_brief.md`
