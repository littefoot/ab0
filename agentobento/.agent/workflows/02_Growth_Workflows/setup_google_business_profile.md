# 02.2 Local SEO: Google Business Profile

**Goal:** Get found on Google Maps.

## 🤖 AI Agent Steps
1.  **Keyword Research:** Identify "near me" keywords for the business type.
2.  **Description Generation:** Write a 750-character SEO-optimized business description.

## 👤 Human Steps
1.  **Claim Profile:** Go to [google.com/business](https://google.com/business).
2.  **Verification:** Complete the video or postcard verification.
3.  **Photo Upload:** Upload 10+ high-quality photos (Interior, Exterior, Team).
4.  **Review Campaign:** Email 5 past customers asking for a review.

## 📄 Deliverable
-   Live Google Maps Listing.
