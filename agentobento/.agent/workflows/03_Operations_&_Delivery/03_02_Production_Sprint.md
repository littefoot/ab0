# Operations: Production Sprint Cycle

**Goal:** Consistent, high-quality output every 2 weeks.

## ♻️ The Cycle
1.  **Sprint Planning (Monday):**
    -   Review `roadmap.json`.
    -   Move items from "Backlog" to "Active Sprint".
    -   Assign owners in Agento Bento.
2.  **Daily Standup (Async):**
    -   What did I do yesterday?
    -   What am I doing today?
    -   Blockers?
3.  **Review & Demo (Friday week 2):**
    -   Show the work.
    -   Client approval.
4.  **Retrospective:**
    -   What went well?
    -   What broke?
