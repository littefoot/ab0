# 03.2 Client Handover

**Goal:** Instant psychological ownership.

## 👤 Human Steps
1.  **Record Walkthrough:** Use Loom/Screen Studio to record a ~3 min tour of their new Agento Bento setup.
2.  **The "Keys" Meeting:**
    -   Show them how to "Zoom" in/out of their business.
    -   Show them where their files live.
    -   Show them the Chatbot (Architect) that knows their strategy.

## 📄 Deliverable
-   The "Master" Zip file or Repository Access.
