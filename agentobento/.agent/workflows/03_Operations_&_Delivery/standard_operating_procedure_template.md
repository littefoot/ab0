# 03.3 Universal SOP Template

**Goal:** Eliminate "Key Person Risk" by documenting how things are done.

## 📝 The Structure
Every SOP must contain:

1.  **Goal:** What is the desired outcome?
2.  **Triggers:** When do we do this? (e.g., "Every Friday," "When a client emails").
3.  **Tools:** What software/hardware is needed?
4.  **The Steps:** Numbered list (Video is preferred).
5.  **Success Check:** How do we know it was done right?

## 🤖 AI Agent Steps
1.  **Drafting:** Feed a transcript of a Loom video to the AI to auto-generate the step-by-step list.
