# 00.3 Proposal Generation (The "McKinsey" Pitch)

**Goal:** Move from "Vendor" to "Strategic Partner".

## 🤖 AI Agent Steps
1.  **Draft Proposal Structure:**
    -   Use the *Problem-Agitate-Solve* framework.
    -   *Crucial:* Do not sell "Deliverables" (e.g., "5 blog posts"). Sell "Outcomes" (e.g., "Dominating organic search for X").
2.  **Generate Concept Art:**
    -   Create a "Future State" diagram (like the Agento Bento concept art) showing their chaotic business turned into an organized machine.

## 👤 Human Steps
1.  **The "Live" Review:** Do not email the PDF. Book a 15-min call to "walk through the strategy."
2.  **Present the "Bento Box":** Show them a screenshot of *their* potential Agento Bento project. "This is your future business. Organized. Visual. Ready."
