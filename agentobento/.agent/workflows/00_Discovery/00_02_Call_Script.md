# 00.2 The Discovery Call Script (The Diagnosis)

**Goal:** Disqualify early or build massive value. Do not "pitch" yet. Diagnose.

## 🗣️ The Script (Human)

### The Opener
"I’ve done some digging. I see you’re [Current State]. Usually, that means you’re struggling with [predicted pain point]. Is that fair, or am I way off?"

### The "Magic Wand" Question
"If we were sitting here 12 months from now with a bottle of champagne, what exactly happened to make us celebrate?"

### The "Cost of Inaction" (Spin Selling)
"If you *don't* fix this by next quarter, what happens? Who gets fired? What revenue is lost?"

### The Budget Anchor
"We usually build 'Digital HQs' in the range of $15k - $50k depending on complexity. Is that a conversation stopper?"

## 📝 Outputs
-   Take notes directly into `00_Discovery/transcript_[client_name].md`.
-   Mark key "pain words" used by the client to repeat back to them later.
