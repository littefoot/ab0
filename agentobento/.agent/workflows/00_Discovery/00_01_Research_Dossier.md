# 00.1 Pre-Discovery Research (The Dossier)

**Goal:** Walk into the first call knowing more about the client than they know about themselves.

## 🤖 AI Agent Steps
1.  **Ingest Target Info:** (Human provides URL/Name).
2.  **Deep Web Search:**
    -   *Corporate health:* Funding rounds, recent hiring sprees (or layoffs), revenue leaks.
    -   *Digital footprint:* SEO gaps, social sentiment, broken user journeys.
    -   *Decision Makers:* LinkedIn profiles of stakeholders (Check for "new in role" - high urgency).
3.  **Generate "The Dossier":**
    -   Create `00_Discovery/dossier_[client_name].md`.
    -   List top 3 predicted pain points.
    -   Draft 5 "Killer Questions" specific to their industry.

## 👤 Human Steps
1.  **Review the Dossier:** Absorb the predicted pain points.
2.  **Hypothesis Check:** Decide on the primary angle (e.g., "They are growing too fast and breaking things" vs "They are stagnant and invisible").
