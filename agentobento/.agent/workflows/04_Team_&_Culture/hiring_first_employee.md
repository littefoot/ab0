# 04.1 Hiring Your First Employee

**Goal:** Duplicate yourself.

## 🤖 AI Agent Steps
1.  **Job Description:** Generate a JD based on the "SOPs" you want to offload.
2.  **Interview Script:** Create 5 scenario-based questions to test competence, not just knowledge.

## 👤 Human Steps
1.  **Post:** LinkedIn, Indeed, or local boards.
2.  **Filter:** Reject anyone who doesn't follow the "Easter Egg" instruction in the JD.
3.  **Trial Task:** Paid 2-hour test project before hiring.
