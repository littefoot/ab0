**Website Design Overview | Canadian Geological Foundation** 

Thank you for the meeting the other day where we discussed your present website and some action items that you would like to see on a newly designed website. This conversation also included using the benefits of technology, new images and possible video that would better describe the CGF story 

and all of the wonderful projects that you have partnered with over the years. We would also want to simplify the layout and navigation so that people landing on the website to apply for grants can do so with ease. We would also like to position the ability to donate for those looking to support the foundation, by having “call to action” tabs and form fills available throughout the website. As mentioned we really need to showcase the success stories of all of the projects the CGF was able to fund, which we can do so with imaging and video. The present website is very large and can be overwhelming to navigate when looking for specific needs or to learn more about the foundation or to submit a grant application. Our suggestion would be to unveil your key positioning pieces on a streamlined home page scroll, which has the ability to tell your story and then allow the user to jump in further only when they are ready to learn more. The majority of what they are needing will be presented to the user on the home page, so they have a true understanding prior to clicking other tabs. To be honest this will also assist with the grant application process and authenticate your relationship with the grant applicants, as they will feel comfortable and have gained trust of the foundation. Using such words as **authenticity, pedigree, quality, trust, professional and community** as we describe the “the who” and “the why” of the Canadian Geological Foundation. With that being said our intention is to design a modern website that is interactive to the user allowing for a positive experience. It will be laid out well and streamlined navigation achieving all of the priority goals of; grant applications, donations and project showcase. Let’s have a look…  
**Project Scope** 

1\. Website Redesign and Modernization 

○ Update 15 year old website to a more modern design and easy to navigate ○ Have a more user friendly experience where they can easily add, remove or change all content 

○ Implement a home page scroll design that tells the story of the CGF, highlighting its mission, successful projects, and the impact of donations and grants. 

2\. Simplify Grant Process 

○ Ensure the grant application process is easy to find and prominently featured on the new website. 

○ Design intuitive forms and clear calls to action to facilitate the application process. 

○ Possible integration of forms into a "customer" management system; or possible built-in CMS 

3\. Enable Online Donations 

○ Integrate a secure and straightforward online donation system. 

○ Promote the donation option effectively on visually appealing pages to encourage contributions from visitors. 

4\. Showcase Successful Projects 

○ Highlight successful projects in a visually appealing "portfolio"- like overview with: 

i. Ability to click into each project to view details on project and related links 

ii. Use new images and videos provided by CGF to create engaging and visually appealing content and/or source images and videos from stock libraries 

5\. Transfer Content and Downsizing 

○ Transfer relevant text content from the existing site to the new site. ○ Reduce the overall size of the website by streamlining content and improving navigation. 

**Project Roadmap** 

1\. Initial Consultation 

○ Conduct a detailed consultation with all team members to understand specific requirements and preferences. 

○ Decide on a general "feel" or "vibe" with a moodboard. 

2\. Content Review 

○ Review and organize existing content to determine what needs to be transferred and what can be updated or removed. 

3\. Design and Development 

○ Create wireframes and design mockups for the new website layout.  
○ Develop the website using a modern, responsive design framework to ensure compatibility with all devices. 

4\. Grant Application and Donation Integration 

○ Implement user-friendly forms and secure payment gateways for grant applications and online donations. 

○ Integrate into the CMS system? 

5\. Testing and Quality Assurance 

○ Conduct thorough testing to ensure all functionalities work seamlessly. ○ Optimize website performance for fast loading times and a smooth user experience. 

6\. Launch and Support 

○ Launch the new website and provide post-launch support to address any issues or updates needed.
