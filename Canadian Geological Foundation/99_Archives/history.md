# CGF History & Context

## Origin
*   **Founded:** 1968 as a charitable organization.
*   **Seed Funding:** Initial grant from J. Thayer Lindsley.
*   **Mission:** Furtherance of geoscience in Canada.

## Impact (as of 2020)
*   **Total Awarded:** > $3.9 Million CAD.
*   **Projects Supported:** > 500 projects.
*   **Focus:** Education, outreach, and awareness building.
