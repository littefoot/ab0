# Application Process

## Deadlines
*   **Spring Round:** March 31st (Recurring annually).
*   **Fall Round:** October 31st (New deadline introduced in 2023).


## Submission Method
*   **Format:** Electronic submission.
*   **Recipient:** The Secretary (secretary@canadiangeologicalfoundation.org - *Verify email*).
*   **Required Documents:**
    1.  Cover Form (`CGF_coverform_word_updated_protected`)
    2.  Application Form (`cgf-word-application-form-2021`)
    3.  Budget Chart
    4.  Summary Page

## Review Process
1.  **Submission:** Received by Secretary.
2.  **Appraisal:** Grants Committee appraises on merit and tier availability.
3.  **Approval:**
    *   Annual Meeting (Grants Committee recommendations).
    *   Board of Directors Spring Meeting (Final Approval - usually coincides with GAC-MAC).
4.  **Notification:** Within one month of the Directors' meeting.
5.  **Disbursement:** Paid upon receipt of paid invoices at successful completion of the project.

## Constraints
*   **Limit:** Maximum 5 pages (excluding forms?). Material in excess will not be circulated.
*   **Incomplete Applications:** Will not be returned for correction.
