# CGF Grant Guidelines

## Purpose
The Canadian Geological Foundation (CGF) invites proposals for grants that support activities of national interest and broad significance to Canadian geoscience, emphasizing long-term importance.

## Grant Tiers

### 1. Small Grants (<$10,000)
*   **Target:** ~30% of annual quota.
*   **Description:** The historical norm. Suitable for smaller, distinct projects.
*   **Criteria:** Same rigorous criteria as larger grants.

### 2. Medium Grants ($10,001 - $30,000)
*   **Target:** ~45% of annual quota.
*   **Description:** For proposals of a more substantial nature.
*   **Availability:** Fewer available than small grants.

### 3. Large Grants (Up to $45,000)
*   **Target:** One per year.
*   **Description:** For large-scale projects by institutions or associations with proven capability.
*   **Requirements:** rigorous application and detailed budget.

### 4. Multi-Year Grants
*   **Description:** Approval for up to 4 years of funding.
*   **Requirements:** Strong rationale, clear milestones, and distinct deliverables per year.
*   **Evaluation:** rigorous separate application process + proven track record.

## Selection Criteria
Priority is given to projects that:
1.  Promote public interest in the value of geological sciences.
2.  Train high school teachers in earth sciences.
3.  Support national seminars/conferences on resource development.
4.  Provide career education materials.
5.  Support publication of special scientific papers (national cooperation).
6.  Produce general geology textbooks, displays, videos, or films.
7.  Involve geological societies in cooperative projects.

*Note: CGF does NOT fund equipment (unless exclusive to project) or salaries (except temporary stipends).*
