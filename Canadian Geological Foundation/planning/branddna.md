# Brand DNA: Canadian Geological Foundation

## Core Identity
*   **Name:** Canadian Geological Foundation (CGF)
*   **Essence:** Unearthing Canada's Future | Where Wonder Fuels Impact.
*   **Mission:** To cultivate a deeper understanding and appreciation of Earth sciences in Canada by strategically funding impactful geoscience education, research, and outreach initiatives that reveal the wonders of our planet.

## Pillars & Values
1.  **Impact:** Driving meaningful advancements and measurable outcomes.
2.  **Engagement:** Fostering curiosity and the pursuit of knowledge.
3.  **Wonder:** Celebrating the awe-inspiring nature of Earth sciences.
4.  **Education:** Championing learning and knowledge-sharing.
5.  **Stewardship:** Responsibly managing resources.
6.  **Collaboration:** Building connections.
7.  **Canadian Focus:** Dedication to Canada's unique landscape.

## Visual Identity (Codebase Specs)

### Colors
**Primary Palette (Dark & Earthy)**
*   **Soil Black:** `#1a191c` (Primary Backgrounds / Darkest Elements)
*   **Charcoal:** `#2e3034` (Secondary Backgrounds / Buttons)
*   **Slate:** `#343A40` (Icons / Text)
*   **Red Accent:** `#ed1c24` (Highlights / Borders)

**Secondary Palette (Accents & Warmth)**
*   **CGF Gold:** `#EAC48D` (Primary Accent / Borders / Highlights)
*   **Muted Gold:** `#D1AC76` (Icons / subtle details)
*   **Glacial Blue:** `rgba(110,158,213,0.99)` (Overlay / Glassmorphism)

**Neutral Palette**
*   **White:** `#ffffff`
*   **Off-White:** `#f8f8f8`
*   **Light Grey:** `#ebebeb`
*   **Soft Grey:** `#f4f4f4`

### Typography
*   **Headings (Display):** `Montserrat Alternates` (ExtraBold) & `Montserrat` (Bold/SemiBold)
*   **Body:** `Manrope` (Regular/SemiBold) & `Work Sans` (Regular/Medium/SemiBold)

### UI Design Tokens
*   **Shapes:**
    *   **Buttons/Tags:** `rounded-[100px]` (Pill shape)
    *   **Cards/Containers:** `rounded-[18px]` or `rounded-[8px]`
*   **Effects:**
    *   **Glassmorphism:** `backdrop-blur-[18px]` with variable opacity backgrounds.
    *   **Depth:** Inner shadows for "pressed" or "inset" look: `shadow-[inset_0px_4px_4px_0px_rgba(135,123,123,0.25)]`.
    *   **Drop Shadows:** Soft outer shadows: `shadow-[0px_4px_4px_0px_rgba(0,0,0,0.25)]`.
*   **Gradients:**
    *   **Subtle Surface:** `linear-gradient(179.4deg, #ffffff 1.2%, #f2f2f2 122.6%)`

## Tone of Voice
*   **Authoritative & Credible**
*   **Inspiring & Passionate**
*   **Accessible & Supportive**
*   **Story-Driven & Evocative**
