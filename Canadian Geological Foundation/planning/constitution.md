# Business Constitution: Canadian Geological Foundation (CGF)

## 1. Core Identity
**Mission:** To cultivate a deeper understanding and appreciation of Earth sciences in Canada by strategically funding impactful geoscience education, research, and outreach initiatives that reveal the wonders of our planet.

**Vision:** To support geoscience projects that respond to the educational needs of the geoscience community and the general public in Canada.

**North Star (Primary Goal):**
**"Launch the World-Class CGF Website."**
*Target:* A platform that attracts quality applications and inspires wonder.

**Secondary:** Maintain the fund through donations.

## 2. Business Model
**Type:** **Option D: The Philanthropic Engine (Non-Profit / Foundation)**
*   **Operating Model:** We are a charitable organization that awards grants to "deserving projects" (Education, Outreach, Research).
*   **History:** Inc. 1968, seeded by J. Thayer Lindsley. >$3.9M awarded to >500 projects.

**Revenue Engine:**
*   **Primary:** Investment income from the Endowment Fund (Initially seeded by J. Thayer Lindsley).
*   **Secondary:** Donations from benefactors (to foster geoscience in Canada).

**Customer Avatar:**
*   **Primary (The Applicants):** Students, Educators, Researchers, and Outreach Groups seeking funding.
*   **Secondary (The Donors):** Benefactors wanting to support Canadian Geoscience.

**Team Topology:**

*   **The Approver (Executive):** Kevin - Provides final sign-off.
